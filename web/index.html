Quick Start Guide

  • Step 1: Go to Scan Videos and choose a folder containing video files (MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WMV, FLV, WebM, and more).
  • Step 2: Click Start Scan. The offline YOLO11n model analyzes sampled frames, logs timestamps, and captures annotated snapshots plus person close-ups.
  • Step 3: Open History & Results to review which videos had people, inspect exact timestamps, and click any snapshot to play the video right at that detected moment.
